*This guide will help you get up and running with the pack.*

## Your first steps

### Adding your first effect

1. Open **Stylized FX Pack → Assets → Prefabs/\*** folder.
2. Drag-and-drop an effect onto your scene.

## Customizing an effect

In the inspector, find the **Particle System** component.

### Changing color of an effect

1. Under the **Particle System** component, find the `Start Color`.
2. Change the color to whatever you want.

*Note: butterfly FXs has the color set under the **Custom Data** module.*

### Changing spawning area

1. Under the **Particle System** component, find the **Shape** module.
2. Expand it.
3. Enable the gizmos of the shape and move the area to where you want the effect to be.
