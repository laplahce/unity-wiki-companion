*The core system. Handles spawning, dropping, collisions and merging into the next candy in the chain.*

## How it works

1. The player system asks for the next candy and holds it above the box.
2. On drop, physics takes over and the candy falls.
3. When two candies of the same type touch, both are released back to the pool and the next candy in the chain spawns at the contact point.

## Adding a candy

Duplicate an existing candy prefab, change its visuals and size, then add it to the chain in the [Candy settings](./settings-candy).
