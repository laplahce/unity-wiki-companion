*Learn to use the player settings.*

> Every setting has a tooltip you can read if you hover over it for a couple of seconds.

## How it works

1. Open **Candy Merge → _Config → Player**.
2. The **Player Settings** is simply a central object used for references to other settings.
3. The **Movement Settings** is simply the settings for player movement.
