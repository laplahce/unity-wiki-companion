*Learn to use the universal settings.*

> Every setting has a tooltip you can read if you hover over it for a couple of seconds.

## How it works

1. Open **Candy Merge → _Config → Settings → Resources**.
2. **Editor Settings** are editor-specific settings such as what the starting scene should be whenever you press play **in the editor only**. This can be an effective way to test the game without needing to switch back to, for example, the menu scene every time before pressing play in the editor.
> This does not affect builds of the game.
3. **Game Settings** is currently empty, but allows you to expand upon it as needed.
4. **Runtime Settings** is a collection of settings that will be read at runtime.
