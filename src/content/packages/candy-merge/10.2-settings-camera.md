*Framing, follow behaviour and zoom for the play area. Found under **Candy Merge → _Config → Camera**.*

## What you can change

- **Framing** - how much padding is left around the box on each side.
- **Follow** - whether the camera tracks the highest candy and how smoothly.
- **Zoom** - the min and max orthographic size, plus the aspect ratio the framing is authored for.

## Tips

Set the framing on the narrowest device you support first, then confirm on wider ones. Every property has a tooltip in the inspector.
