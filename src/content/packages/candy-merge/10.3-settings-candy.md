*The candy chain, sizes, spawn weights and merge results. Found under **Candy Merge → _Config → Candy**.*

## The chain

The chain is an ordered list. Each entry points at a prefab and defines what it merges into, so adding a candy is a prefab plus a list entry.

## Spawn weights

Only the first few entries are usually droppable. Weights control how often each one is picked.

## Merge results

Merging two identical candies spawns the next entry in the chain and awards its score value.
