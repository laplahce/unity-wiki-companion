*Owns the play area: the box, its bounds and the objects that make up a round.*

## Responsibilities

- Builds the box and its collision bounds from the size in the runtime settings.
- Keeps a reference to every live candy so a round can be cleared instantly.
- Resets the area between rounds without reloading the scene.
