*This guide will help you get the template running and make it yours.*

## Your first steps

### Play the game

1. Complete the [Installation guide](./installation) first.
2. Open **Candy Merge → Scenes → MENU**.
3. Press play and finish a round to see the full loop: menu, level, game over, save.

### Change your first setting

1. Open **Candy Merge → _Config → Settings → Resources**.
2. Select `Runtime Settings`.
3. Change something small, such as the drop cooldown or the box size.
4. Press play again to see the change immediately.
5. For the full list, see [Settings & configuration](./settings-and-configuration).

### Add your own candy

1. Open **Candy Merge → Systems → Candies → Prefabs** and duplicate the `Candy` prefab.
2. Swap its sprite and adjust its collider to match.
3. Register it in the candy list inside the candy configuration so it joins the merge chain.
4. For more information, check out [Systems](./systems).

#### A tip:

Every property in the inspector has a tooltip attached. Hover a field for a couple of seconds to see what it does before changing it.
