*Input, drop position limits and drop timing. Found under **Candy Merge → _Config → Player**.*

## What you can change

- **Input** - pointer, drag or keyboard control and the sensitivity of each.
- **Drop bounds** - the left and right limits the candy can be moved between.
- **Drop cooldown** - how long the player waits between two drops.
