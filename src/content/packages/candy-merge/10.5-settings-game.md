*Round rules, scoring and game over thresholds. Found under **Candy Merge → _Config → Game**.*

## What you can change

- **Scoring** - the score awarded per merge and any combo multiplier.
- **Game over** - the height a candy has to stay above, and for how long, before the round ends.
- **Round flow** - what is reset between rounds and what carries over.
