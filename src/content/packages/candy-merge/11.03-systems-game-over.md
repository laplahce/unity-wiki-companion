*Detects the losing condition, stops the round and shows the result screen.*

## How it works

1. A trigger sits above the box. A candy resting inside it for longer than the grace period ends the round.
2. Input is disabled and physics is frozen.
3. The result screen fades in with the score, and a screenshot of the final board can be captured.

Thresholds and the grace period live in the [Game settings](./settings-game).
