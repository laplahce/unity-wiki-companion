*The runtime glue: initialization order, references between systems and the pooled object lifetime.*

## Initialization order

1. Settings and save data are loaded.
2. Pools are filled from the Pool object.
3. Systems register themselves and the round starts.

Keep new systems in this order rather than relying on Unity's `Awake` order.
