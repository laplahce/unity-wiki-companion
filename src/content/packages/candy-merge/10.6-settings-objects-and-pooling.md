*Two ScriptableObjects under **Candy Merge → Resources** manage prefabs at runtime.*

## Objects

Prefabs that any script can request at any time, looked up by key. Use it for one-off spawns such as popups or the game over screen.

## Pool

Prefabs that are pooled during initialization instead of being instantiated on demand. Candies and particles belong here.

> Pool sizes that are too small fall back to instantiation, which is safe but allocates. Raise the count instead.
