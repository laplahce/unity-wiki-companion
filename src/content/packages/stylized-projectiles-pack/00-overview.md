**Stylized Projectiles Pack** is a pack of stylized projectiles. Each effect has a muzzle flash & hit effect included.

## What's inside

- **19** projectiles.
- **19** muzzle flashes, **19** hit effects.
- **Modular** projectile script - any custom movement.
- **8** shaders (heat distortion, 6-way lighting smoke, ice, +more).
- 160+ textures.
- Camera shake controller.
