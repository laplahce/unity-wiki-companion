**Customizable Rocks Pack** is a pack of 100 customizable rocks with a robust shader to control everything.

## What's inside

- Color with **gradients**
- **100** rocks
- Custom shader
- **LODs**
- **Procedural** moss, weathering, +more
- Flat & shaded variants
