**Stylized Foliage Creator** is a tool to create stylized bushes, foliage & clouds. It also includes trees, bushes & clouds ready out-of-the-box.

## What's inside

- **Foliage creator** with realtime editing
- Color with **gradients**
- **40+** prefabs (x9 trees, x25 bushes & x11 clouds)
Foliage & surface shader - dither fading, wind, stylized light, weathering +more
- **30+** noise textures
- **10+** foliage textures
