*This guide will help you get up and running with the pack.*

## Your first steps

1. Open the **Stylized Tree Pack → Demo → Demo** scene.
2. Take a look around at the trees, bushes and clouds.

## Adding trees to your worlds

1. Open **Stylized Tree Pack → Assets → Prefabs**.
2. Find a prefab you like and drag-and-drop it into your scene.
3. It works out-of-the-box.

## Wind setup

1. Add the **Wind Controller** component to any object in the scene via **Component → STP → Wind Controller**.
2. Assign a **Wind Noise** to the component.
3. Rotate the object to control wind direction.
