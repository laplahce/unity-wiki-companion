## Your first steps

### Play the game

> You can start the game from either the `Menu` or the `Level` scenes.
1. To see the entire game flow, open **Candy Merge → Scenes → MENU**.
2. Press play and finish a round to see the full loop: `Menu`, `Level`, `Game Over`, `Save`.

### Change your first setting

1. Open **Candy Merge → _Config → Settings → Resources**.
2. Select `Runtime Settings`.
3. Change something small, such as the drop cooldown.
4. Press play again to see the change immediately.
5. For the full list, see [Settings & configuration](./settings).

> To understand specific areas of the template, find them in this documentation and read more about it in detail.

#### A tip:

> Every property in the inspector has a tooltip attached. Hover a field for a couple of seconds to see what it does before changing it.
