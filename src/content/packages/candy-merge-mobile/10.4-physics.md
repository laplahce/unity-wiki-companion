*Learn to use the physics settings.*

> Every setting has a tooltip you can read if you hover over it for a couple of seconds.

## How it works

1. Open **Candy Merge → _Config → Physics**.
> The physics settings are using Unity's own **Physics Material** and can therefore simply be changed like usual.
2. For example, you can open one of the physics materials and change its friction to see what changes (e.g. the friction between two candies can be changed via the **Candy** physics material).
