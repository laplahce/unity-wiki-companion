*Quick-reference for which versions, render pipelines & platforms the template is compatible with.*

## Notes

- Unity 6 is recommended; earlier versions need one physics tweak described in the [Installation guide](./installation).
- HDRP is not supported. The template is 2D and ships Built-In and URP materials only.
- `TextMeshPro`, the new `Input System` and `Shader Graph` are required dependencies.
