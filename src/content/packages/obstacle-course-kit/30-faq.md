## Common questions

## Which render pipelines are supported?

URP and HDRP. Follow the [Installation guide](./installation) to learn how to use the pack with your render pipeline. [For more compatibility information, refer to the Compatibility page](./compatibility).

## Which version of Unity are supported?

2021, 2022, 2023, 6.0 and later. Follow the [Installation guide](./installation) to learn how to use the pack with your Unity version. [For more compatibility information, refer to the Compatibility page](./compatibility).

## Which platforms are supported?

Platforms that support VFX Graph are supported (e.g. mobile & WebGL isn't supported). [For more compatibility information, refer to the Compatibility page](./compatibility).

## Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

Yes. The licence covers commercial projects, per the standard Unity Asset Store EULA.

## Can I use the assets in my 2D game?

Yes. Although, the pack was designed for 3D.

## Can I use the assets in my 3D game?

Yes.
