**Obstacle Course Kit** is a platformer kit for obstacle courses & party games for Unity. It also includes drag-and-drop obstacles, platforms, effects, all with customizable colors to build your courses.

::youtube{id=X8e9PoW1lyc caption="Video tutorial for the pack. A great choice for those who prefer learning by video."}

## What's inside

- **470** assets.
- **20+** Effects.
- Customizable colors.
- **7** sample levels.
- **Plug-and-play** attributes.
- **Drag-and-drop** obstacles.
