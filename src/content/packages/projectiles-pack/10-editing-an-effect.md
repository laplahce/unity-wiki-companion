*Learn how to edit & customize effects using the custom editor.*

## Editing your first effect

1. In the inspector, find the **Projectile FX**, **Muzzle FX** or **Hit FX** component.
2. Click **Edit FX**.
3. Navigate through the components making up the effect using the tabs at the top (e.g. *Emitter*).
4. Try to change something (e.g. the density of the particles).
5. You can always add more components to the effect. For this, navigate to any of the tabs.
6. Scroll down to **Particles* and add one using a preset or an empty one.
