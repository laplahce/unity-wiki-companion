## Which Unity versions are supported?

The supported range is listed in the overview infobox. In general, I target the current Unity LTS plus the two previous LTS releases.

## Does DOTween work in WebGL / mobile / consoles?

WebGL, iOS and Android are tested in CI. Console targets compile but I cannot test them on every patch — ping me before shipping if you depend on console support.

## Can I use DOTween in a commercial project?

Yes. The license is the standard Unity Asset Store EULA — one seat per developer, unlimited shipped projects.

## How do I report a bug?

Open the [contact page](/contact) with your Unity version, the DOTween version, the platform you're targeting, and a minimal reproduction project.
