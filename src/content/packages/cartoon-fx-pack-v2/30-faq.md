## Which render pipelines are supported?

Built-in, URP and HDRP. The importer detects the active pipeline and installs the
matching material set.

## Can I use the effects in a commercial game?

Yes. A single asset licence covers commercial projects, per the standard Unity
Asset Store EULA.

## Can I edit the included effects, not just create new ones?

Yes. Every shipped effect has a preset asset, so it opens in the FX creator the
same way an effect you authored does.

## Do the comic text effects support custom fonts?

Yes. The sprite pop layer accepts a TextMesh Pro asset, so any font in your
project can be used for callouts.

## When is it released?

The pack is still in development and has not been submitted for Asset Store
review yet.
