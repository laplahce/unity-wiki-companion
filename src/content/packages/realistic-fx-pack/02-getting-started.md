## Your first steps

### Adding your first effect

1. Open **Realistic FX Pack → Assets → Prefabs/\*** folder.
2. Drag-and-drop an effect onto your scene.

### Adding a curve effect

1. Open **Realistic FX Pack → Assets → Prefabs → Curve Samples** folder.
2. Drag-and-drop an effect onto your scene.
3. Use the gizmos to move the path around.
4. Try to keep the lines between the handles a green color (this will result in the particles moving in a consistent speed).
5. In the inspector, add and remove points and try to shape the path to your environment.
