*This guide will help you get up and running with the pack.*

## Your first steps

1. Open the **Stylized Rocks → Demo → Demo** scene.
2. Take a look around at the trees, bushes and clouds.

## Adding rocks to your worlds

1. Open **Stylized Rocks → Assets → Prefabs**.
2. Find a prefab you like and drag-and-drop it into your scene.
3. It works out-of-the-box.

## Changing the texture style

1. Select the material of the rock you want to restyle.
2. Find the corresponding texture under **Stylized Rocks → Shared → Source → Textures → Albedos**.
3. Each texture is numbered to match a specific rock - find the one with the same number and size (normal or small).
4. Apply the texture to the **Base Map** of the material.
