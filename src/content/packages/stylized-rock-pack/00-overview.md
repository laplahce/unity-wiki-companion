**Stylized Rock Pack** is a pack of stylized, hand-sculpted rocks.

## What's inside

- **20** rocks.
- **LODs**.
- Mobile-ready.
- **6 variants** per rock (mossy, snowy +more).
- Normal & occlusion maps from high poly variants.
