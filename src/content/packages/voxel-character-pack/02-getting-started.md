*This guide will help you get up and running with the pack.*

## Your first steps

### Checking out the pack

1. Open the **Voxel Character Pack → Demo → Demo** scene.
2. Play it.
3. Use the buttons on the top to navigate between characters.
4. Use `[W]` & `[S]` to move, `[LEFT-SHIFT]` to sprint & `[SPACE]` to jump.

*The included player & camera controller in the demo scene is purely for demonstration purposes. I would suggest creating your own controllers, but feel free to use them.*

### Checking out a character

1. Open **Voxel Character Pack → Assets → Prefabs** folder.
2. Drag-and-drop an effect onto your scene.
