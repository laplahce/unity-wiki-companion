*Quick-reference for which versions, render pipelines & platforms the pack is compatible with.*

## Notes

- The pack works with all platforms & versions.
- The only version & pipeline dependent assets are the materials. To make it work with your specific setup, make sure the materials under **Voxel Character Pack → Assets → Materials** are compatatible with your environment.
